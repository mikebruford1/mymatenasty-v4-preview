Keep it real. Keep it kind.

01

Ask before posting

Only share photographs you are entitled to share, especially where somebody may reasonably expect privacy.

02

No humiliation

Do not use the platform to bully, intimidate, shame or target another person.

03

No illegal content

Illegal, exploitative, hateful, violent or infringing material is not permitted.

04

Respect removal

If somebody shown in a photograph raises a genuine privacy concern, take it seriously.
